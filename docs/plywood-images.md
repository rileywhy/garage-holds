# Plywood images

The sheets in `public/images/plywood` sit behind holds on the pages, under
and behind them in the 3D viewer, and in the pile's STL previews. The wood
looks the same in the light and dark themes.

## Generated images

No freely licensed photo of the back of a board turned up, so these were
generated with OpenAI image generation through the Codex CLI. They are not
photographs of a real board or room.

The board came out with a hole at every crossing of its pencilled layout
grid. A gym drills every other crossing, so the rest were painted out with
wood copied from beside them. Taking one pencilled square as 100 mm, that
leaves holes 200 mm apart along a row, in rows 100 mm apart and offset by
100 mm, drawn at true size in the 3D viewer.

| File               | What it is                                                               |
| ------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| `board-front.webp` | The climbing side of a pine plywood board, drilled as above              |
| `board-back.webp`  | The back of the same board: four-prong T-nuts hammered in                |
| `bench.webp`       | 300 mm square of the front, with a hole 100 mm from the centre each way  |
| `panel-front.webp` | 256 mm square of the front, centred on a hole (the viewer's board)       |
| `panel-back.webp`  | The matching square of the back, mirrored, as seen from behind           |
| `table.webp`       | 600 × 400 mm of the front, cut along lines of holes so it tiles mirrored |
| `wall.webp`        | An out-of-focus garage climbing wall, behind the pile's STL previews     |
