# Garage Holds

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users and purpose

Climbers, home-wall owners, routesetters and makers discover, design and print
climbing holds. The catalog pairs files with creator/community printing recipes;
the labs provide adjustable geometry and STL export. This context comes from
the existing application and the owner's RIL-95 brief; no additional audience
or commercial claims are inferred.

## Workflows and constraints

Browse and download publicly. Accounts enable uploads, reviews, bookmarks and
saved presets. Existing Supabase/R2 configuration and permissions remain the
authority. Labs retain every parameter, control, camera, editor, geometry and
export behavior. Safety copy, units, legal terms and factual claims stay intact.
The website must render when backend credentials are absent.

## Brand commitments

Garage Holds; the finalized RIL-59 SVG artwork is immutable. Direct, practical
copy for people making holds. The owner authorized a replacement visual world:
industrial workshop, climbing gym and precision CAD. No functional rewrite.

## Implementation

Existing Next.js/React/TypeScript, shared CSS, feature modules, Python geometry.
Keep the existing branch and uncommitted work. No unnecessary dependencies.
The owner delegates design decisions and requests no approval between phases.
RIL-95 remains open for review; do not automatically complete it.
